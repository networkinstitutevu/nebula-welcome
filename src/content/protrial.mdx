# Introduction

Nebula offers different types of user accounts: Pro and Trial. This section explains the differences between these two types of users, including their features, limitations, and benefits.

#### **Trial Level Users**
As a Trial Level user you get unlimited access to our Trial Level server (check out our [Fair Use Policy](/welcome/fairuse)). This server features powerful enough hardware to offer you a chance to get to know Nebula. With a Trial User account the idea is to test Nebula to see if Nebula is the AI platform for you.
Once you are sure Nebula is the right platform for you, you can upgrade to a paid Pro User account.

Trial Level accounts are completely free of charge. A Trial account lasts for 1 month. At any time you can ask us to upgrade your account to a paid Pro Level account.

You can request a Trial User account on our <a href="https://forms.gle/8ziuLPTWYivdf2em9" target='_blank'>Request Access Form</a>.

#### **Pro Level Users**

As a Pro Level user you get access to our Pro Level server. This server features high-end hardware designed to handle more demanding AI workloads. With a Pro User account, you can fully leverage Nebula's capabilities for your AI projects.

Pro Level accounts are paid account. The pricing and subscription details can be found on our <a href="https://networkinstitute.org/nebula/nebula-pricing/" target='_blank'>Pricing Page</a>.

If you require a customized plan to fit your needs and budget, please do not hesitate to contact us at [nebula@networkinstitute.org](mailto:nebula@networkinstitute.org).
